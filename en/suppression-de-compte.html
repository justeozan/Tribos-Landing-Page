Delete your account

How to request the deletion of your Tribos account and associated data, and what is erased or retained.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Your account is deleted directly in the Tribos mobile app, and the process is automatic. This page describes the steps to follow, the data deleted, the data retained and how long it is kept, in line with the requirements of the app stores.

Delete your account from the app

Deletion requires no action on our part. It is irreversible.

  • Open the Tribos app and sign in to your account.
  • Open Settings: on the home screen, tap your profile picture, then the gear icon at the top right.
  • Scroll down to the “Zone de danger” section and tap “Supprimer le compte”. The app warns you that this action is irreversible.
  • Confirm by typing the word SUPPRIMER in the dialog box, then tap “Supprimer”. Deletion starts immediately: once it is complete, you are signed out and the app returns to the sign-in screen.

If deletion is interrupted, for example because of a lost connection, the app tells you and your account stays active. Some of your data may already have been erased: simply start the deletion again, and it resumes where it stopped. If the failure persists, write to us.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, if deleting from the app fails, or if you are only signed up to the beta waitlist on this site, you can request deletion by email.

  • Write to us at evey.run@gmail.com, from the email address used to create the account, with the subject “Suppression de compte”.
  • Identity check. We verify that the request comes from the account holder.
  • Deletion handled within a maximum of thirty days from receipt of the verified request.

Data deleted

Deleting from the app erases:

  • your sign-in account: email address, password, username saved as the account name, sign-in sessions and preferences, including the list of members you have blocked;
  • your profile: username and profile picture;
  • your statistics, your points and your training progress;
  • the programs you follow and the history of your completed sessions;
  • your group memberships and all the messages you have sent;
  • the groups you own: they are transferred to the longest-standing remaining member, or deleted along with their messages if no other member remains.

Deleting your account does not remove your possible sign-up to the beta waitlist, which is managed separately: to be removed from it, write to us as described above.

Data retained and retention periods

  • Reports. Reports you have made, or that concern you, are kept for as long as they take to process, and for no more than 12 months, for moderation purposes. They may contain a copy of the text of a reported message.
  • Challenges. Challenges you have started or received are not yet erased automatically. They contain no username, no email address and no photo: only technical identifiers, the number of sessions targeted and the week concerned. Write to us to have them erased.
  • Group photos and names. A group’s photo and name remain attached to the group for as long as it exists, even if you are the one who chose them: they are shared elements. If erasing a photo fails, or if a group is deleted, the photo file may remain on our servers without being displayed; write to us to have it erased.
  • Backups and technical logs. Any residual traces in our hosting provider’s backups and technical logs are purged within a maximum of thirty days after deletion.
  • App Store and Google Play accounts. Managing your Apple or Google account, and the data associated with it, falls under Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies, and not under this procedure.
  • Legal obligations. If a legal obligation required us to keep certain data, that data would be limited to the strict minimum required by law.

Your rights

You have, in particular, a right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time. The details of these rights and how to exercise them are described in the privacy policy.

For any question about deleting your account, write to evey.run@gmail.com.
