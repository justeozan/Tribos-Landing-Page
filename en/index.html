How it works

Three things to do once, then the app takes care of the pace.

  1. 01

    Pick your program

    Ten programs, from beginner to advanced: basic strength, HIIT, calisthenics, explosiveness, MMA, running, mobility. You pick yours and follow it, week by week.

  2. 02

    Create or join a group

    A six-character code, that's all it takes. Every week, the group sets a number of sessions: two per person.

  3. 03

    Do your first session

    One exercise at a time, full-screen photo, countdown sets and timed rest. You finish, you earn 50 points, and your session counts for the whole group.

The group

One goal, the whole group.

Each week, the group sets a number of sessions to hit: two per person. Every session you do counts for everyone. You can see where everyone stands, and how many days are left.

It's not a competition between you. Nobody is ranked by their contribution: either the group makes it, or it doesn't.

FAQ

Is Tribos free?

Yes, the beta is free and no credit card is required. We want to see first whether the product holds up with real groups. Any paid offer would be announced in advance.

iOS or Android?

Both. On Android, we let people in wave by wave. On iPhone, the beta runs through TestFlight, Apple's app for betas : pick iPhone when you sign up, and the install link shows up right after.

Do I need equipment?

Not to get started. Calisthenics, running, mobility and HIIT are done with bodyweight, at home. Others, like basic strength, need a barbell or a gym.

Do my friends need the app?

Yes: their sessions count toward the week's goal, so they need the app. Give them your six-character code: they create an account, enter the code, and you're in.

You don't need to have convinced them before signing up. We give you what you need to invite them right after.

What happens when I finish a session?

You earn 50 points, or double if your consistency bonus is active. Most importantly, your session counts toward the whole group's weekly goal.

When is it available?

Tribos is in private beta and access opens in waves. We don't announce a public date : we'd rather open when it's ready.

What happens to my data?

Your email address is only used to tell you when the beta opens. It is not sold. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time.

The details are in the privacy policy.
