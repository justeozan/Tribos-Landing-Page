Why it works in a team

Real connection, not a seminar

People who train together talk to each other differently. The group builds connections across departments and across sites.

A simple framework

One program for the whole team: nothing to organize each week, nothing to negotiate between levels.

Visible momentum

The shared goal and completed sessions make participation visible, without turning sport into an obligation.

How it gets set up

  1. 01

    Create the group

    You create your company’s group and invite teams with a code to share.

  2. 02

    Pick the program

    You pick a program suited to an average level, over a few weeks, and everyone starts on it.

  3. 03

    Let the group take over

    Everyone does their session whenever they like. The shared goal and the group messages do the rest.
