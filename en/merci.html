Got it. Now the real question: who's with you?

Tribos is no good on your own. We open up to groups that are already formed first: every person who signs up with you moves your place in the queue up.

It's already open on iPhone

Install TestFlight, Apple's app for betas, then open this link from your iPhone to install Tribos.

Install the iPhone beta

Send the invite now

You need 2 or 3 people to start a group. The message is already written, you just pick where to send it.

WhatsApp SMS

“I got my spot in the Tribos beta. I need 2 or 3 people to start our group. Join us here: tribos.evey.run/en”

How many of you will be in the group?

This tells us what size of wave to open. You can always add more people later.

What happens next

  • We open access in waves, so we can keep proper track of each group.
  • You'll get an email with your install link and everything you need to set up your group.
  • One email, the one for your access. No newsletter, unsubscribe in one click.
