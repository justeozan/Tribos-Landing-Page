Privacy policy

What Tribos collects, why, and what you can ask for at any time.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy covers two things: the Tribos mobile app, on iPhone and Android, and this website, which collects sign-ups for the beta waiting list. We collect the minimum needed to run the service, and we explain below exactly what, why and for how long.

In short: no advertising, no audience measurement tools, no selling of data. Members of your group see what you share with them in the app, and we only use your data to run Tribos.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is SAHIN Ozan, whose registered office is in Villeurbanne.

Contact for any question about your data: evey.run@gmail.com.

In the Tribos app

The data we collect

  • Your account. Your email address, your password and your username, which also serves as your account name. The password is entrusted to our hosting provider's authentication service, which only keeps a hash of it: it is never stored in plain text.
  • Your profile. Your username, your profile photo if you choose one, and a flag that records that you have completed the app's onboarding.
  • Your workout activity. The sessions you complete (which session, on what date and time, how many points it earned you), your progress in each program, the programs you follow and your statistics: points, level, current streak and best streak, number of sessions and challenges completed.
  • Your groups. The groups you create (name, photo if you add one, invite code, creation date) and those you join (join date, owner or member role).
  • Your messages. The text of the messages you send in your group's chat, with their date.
  • Your challenges. The challenges you send to a member or receive: who sent it, to whom, in which group, how many sessions are targeted and for which week.
  • Your reports. When you report a message or a member, we record your identifier, that of the reported person, the group concerned, what you are reporting (a message or a member), the reason you choose and, for a message, a copy of its text limited to 1,000 characters.
  • Blocked members. The list of identifiers of the members you have blocked, saved in your account preferences.
  • Technical connection data. When you sign in, our hosting provider records session information, such as your IP address, device and system type, and the country inferred from the IP address.

On your phone, the app keeps your sign-in session and remembers that you have already seen the introduction screens.

What we do not collect

No location, no contacts, no advertising identifier. No step count, heart rate or any other sensor data: Tribos does not connect to any health app or health watch. The sessions counted are only the ones you declare yourself by completing a session.

The app does not include any advertising, audience measurement or tracking tools, nor any third-party crash reporting tool.

On Android, it only requests one system permission: access to the Internet. On iPhone, access to your photos or camera is only used at the moment you choose a profile photo or a group photo.

Why we use them

  • Create and secure your account, sign you in and, if you request it, send you a link to reset your password.
  • Run Tribos: track your program, count your sessions and points, calculate your group's weekly goal, display the chat and challenges.
  • Moderation: review reports, remove prohibited content and exclude users who violate the Terms of use.
  • Hide messages from members you have blocked, so you don't see them.
  • Protect the service against fraudulent access and abuse.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not use it to profile you.

What other members of your group see

Tribos is a group app. Members of your groups see your username, your profile photo, your sessions for the week and the last one you completed, your total points, the programs you follow, your messages and the challenges you send them. That is the very principle of the app.

Profile and group photos are served from an unlisted web address: anyone who obtained that address could display the photo, even without a Tribos account. So avoid photos you would not want to see circulating.

Legal basis

  • Performance of the contract, within the meaning of Article 6.1.b of the GDPR, for your account, your profile, your activity, your groups, your messages, your challenges and the list of members you block: without this data, we cannot provide the service described in the Terms of use.
  • Our legitimate interest, within the meaning of Article 6.1.f of the GDPR, for moderation and the handling of reports, account security, downloading font files and looking for app fixes.

How long we keep them

  • Your account and your activity data are kept for as long as your account exists. They are deleted when you delete your account, as described on the Delete your account page.
  • Messages are only displayed for 24 hours in the app, but they remain stored on our servers beyond that period. They are deleted when their author deletes their account, or when the group is deleted. Leaving a group does not delete the messages you sent there.
  • Challenges are not yet deleted automatically, either when an account is deleted or when a group is deleted. They only contain technical identifiers, with no username, email address or photo. Write to us to have them erased.
  • Reports are kept for as long as needed to process them, and for no more than 12 months.
  • The list of members you block is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Technical connection data is kept by our hosting provider for the duration of the session, then in its technical logs, which are deleted through automatic rotation.

On this site: the beta waiting list

The data we collect

When you sign up for the beta waiting list, we collect:

  • Your email address. This is the only information stored on the waiting list.
  • Optional answers. The form may ask you which phone you will use Tribos on (Android or iPhone), and the confirmation page asks how many people will be in your group. These answers are sent to our server along with your address, but they are not stored. Your browser also keeps your address and your phone choice for the length of your visit, in particular to pre-fill the confirmation page.
  • Technical data related to form submission. When the form is submitted, our hosting provider records the date and time of submission, the IP address from which the form was sent and the information transmitted by your browser, as well as the result of the invisible honeypot field intended to block bots. This data is used solely to filter automated and abusive submissions.

We do not collect your name, your phone number, your company, or any health or training data through this site.

Why we collect this data

Your email address is used exclusively to let you know when the Tribos beta opens and to send you the practical information you need to access it, such as an invitation or an access code.

On the same basis, we may send you a limited number of messages about the progress of the beta.

We do not use your address for advertising, and we do not use it to profile you.

The technical form data is used solely for security and to fight spam.

Legal basis

The processing of your email address is based on your consent, within the meaning of Article 6.1.a of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This consent is given freely when you enter your address and submit the form.

You may withdraw your consent at any time, without having to give a reason. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

The processing of anti-spam technical data is based on our legitimate interest in protecting the form against automated submissions, within the meaning of Article 6.1.f of the GDPR.

How long we keep them

Your email address is kept until the beta opens and for as long as needed to support you as a beta tester, and for no more than three years from our last contact with you, in line with the practice recommended by the CNIL (the French data protection authority) for prospecting.

If you request deletion of your address or withdraw your consent, your address is removed from our records as soon as possible, and no later than thirty days.

If the beta project were abandoned, the list would be deleted.

Technical data associated with form submissions is kept by our hosting provider for as long as needed for anti-spam filtering, then deleted along with the corresponding submission.

Who has access to your data

On the Tribos side, only the publisher, SAHIN Ozan, accesses your data, to run the service, manage the beta and handle reports. In the app, members of your groups see what is described above.

We use two processors:

Appwrite Cloud, a data region located in Frankfurt (Germany, European Union), which hosts the app's data (accounts, profiles, activity, groups, messages, challenges, reports and photos) as well as the website, and which runs the processing of the sign-up form. As such, Appwrite also temporarily receives and stores the email address entered in the form, along with the technical data associated with the submission.

Resend, a company based in San Francisco, United States, which stores the waiting list (your email address) and handles the sending of beta messages, including the invitation and the access code.

These processors act on our instructions, under data processing agreements compliant with Article 28 of the GDPR.

The app also relies on two technical services, which receive no data from your account:

  • Google Fonts, a Google service: the app downloads its font files the first time it needs them, which transmits your phone's IP address to Google, as with any connection to a website.
  • Shorebird, a service of Code Town, Inc., a company based in the United States, which lets us fix bugs without going through an app update. At launch, the app asks Shorebird whether a fix is available. This request transmits the app identifier, its version, the platform (iOS or Android), the processor architecture and an anonymous installation identifier, as well as the IP address required for any connection.

Apple and Google also process data related to downloading the app from the App Store or Google Play, under their own privacy policies.

Outside these cases, your data may be disclosed to an administrative or judicial authority if the law requires us to do so.

Transfers outside the European Union

App data is stored in the European Union, in Germany. Hosting of the website and processing of the form also take place there.

Managing the waiting list and sending messages, however, involve a transfer of your email address to the United States, where Resend is based.

This transfer is governed by the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission, included in the processing agreement signed with Resend, and supplemented where applicable by the additional measures provided for in that same agreement.

Requests sent to Google Fonts and Shorebird may also be processed in the United States, where these companies are based.

You can write to evey.run@gmail.com to obtain information about the safeguards applicable to these transfers.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or exchange your data, whether it is your email address or your use of the app.

We do not pass it on to any data broker, advertising network or commercial partner.

It is used solely for the purposes described above.

Your rights

In accordance with the GDPR and the French Data Protection Act (Loi Informatique et Libertés), you have the following rights over your data:

  • Right of access. To know whether we hold data about you and to obtain a copy of it.
  • Right to rectification. To have inaccurate data corrected, such as an email address or a username.
  • Right to erasure. To request deletion of your data, or of your address from the waiting list. You can also delete your account yourself from the app.
  • Right to object. To object to the processing of your data based on our legitimate interest, as well as to messages related to the beta.
  • Right to restriction of processing. To request the temporary freezing of the use of your data.
  • Right to data portability. To receive the data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Right to withdraw your consent. At any time, without giving a reason, which means you are removed from the waiting list.

How to exercise your rights

To delete your Tribos account and the associated data, the procedure to follow is described on the Delete your account page.

For any other request, write to evey.run@gmail.com, stating your request and, if possible, sending it from the email address of your Tribos account or the one used for the waiting list.

We reply within one month of receiving the request. This period may be extended by two months if the request is complex, in which case we will let you know.

We may ask you for proof of identity only in case of reasonable doubt about the identity of the requester.

You may also write to us by post at SAHIN Ozan, Villeurbanne.

Complaint to the CNIL

If, after contacting us, you believe that your rights are not being respected, you may lodge a complaint with the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL, the French data protection authority):

CNIL, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07.

Website: https://www.cnil.fr

Children

The Tribos app is not intended for people under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, write to us at evey.run@gmail.com: we will delete that account and its data.

Cookies and trackers

This site does not set any audience measurement cookie, advertising cookie or tracker for profiling purposes.

Our hosting provider may use cookies that are strictly necessary for the service to function properly, in particular for load balancing and protection against abuse. These cookies are not used to track you from one site to another and do not require your prior consent.

No consent banner is therefore displayed. If an audience analytics tool, an advertising service or any other non-essential tracker were added to the site, this section would be updated and your consent would be requested before any cookie is set.

The app, for its part, uses no trackers: it does not access your phone's advertising identifier and does not track you from one app to another.

Data protection officer

We have not appointed a data protection officer. Appointing one is not mandatory in our situation, because we are not a public body, our core activity does not consist of regular and systematic large-scale monitoring of individuals, and we do not process sensitive data on a large scale within the meaning of Article 9 of the GDPR.

Your requests concerning your data are handled directly at evey.run@gmail.com.

Data security

Exchanges between the app, your browser and our servers are encrypted using HTTPS. Your password is never stored in plain text.

We limit access to service administration and to form submissions to the people who need it, and we protect the accounts concerned.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change along with the app and the website, in particular when Tribos opens to the public or if our hosting provider or tools change.

The version in force is the one published on this page, with its update date. If a material change concerns your data, we will inform you.
