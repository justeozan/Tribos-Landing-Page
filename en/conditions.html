Terms of use

The rules of the Tribos app: what we provide, what we expect from everyone and what happens in case of abuse.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

These terms govern your use of the Tribos mobile app, published by SAHIN Ozan, residing in Villeurbanne (France). By creating an account or using the app, you accept them. If you do not accept them, do not use Tribos.

The service

Tribos is a free app for training as a group. Among other things, it lets you:

  • follow a training program and complete your sessions, guided set by set;
  • create a group with your friends or colleagues, or join one using an invitation code;
  • work together toward a shared session goal for the week;
  • chat in the group chat;
  • challenge other members and earn points.

Tribos is in beta: the app keeps evolving, and some features may change, appear or disappear. For now, it offers no purchases and no subscriptions.

Your account

  • Minimum age. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account.
  • One account, one person. Your account is personal. Choose a username that does not impersonate anyone and that follows the rules of conduct below.
  • A valid email address. Use an address that belongs to you: it is what lets you recover your account.
  • Security. Keep your password to yourself. If you think someone else is using your account, write to us.

Rules of conduct: zero tolerance

Tribos is made to keep you motivated among friends. We enforce a zero-tolerance policy toward objectionable content and abusive users. It is forbidden to post, in a message, a username, a group name or a photo, or to address to another member:

  • harassment, intimidation or insults;
  • hateful or discriminatory remarks, in particular based on origin, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or disability;
  • sexual or pornographic content;
  • threats, or incitement to violence or to self-harm;
  • spam, unsolicited advertising or scam attempts;
  • any illegal content, or content that infringes on others’ rights, such as a photo of a person published without their consent.

It is also forbidden to impersonate another person, to get around a block or an exclusion, to try to access other users’ data, and to disrupt the operation of the service.

Reporting and blocking

In the app, you can:

  • report a message or a member: the report is sent to us with the reason you choose and, for a message, a copy of its text;
  • block a member: their messages are then hidden for you.

You can also report content or behavior to us by writing to evey.run@gmail.com.

Our moderation commitment

We review every report within 24 hours. When content breaks these terms, we remove it and exclude the user who posted it, by suspending or deleting their account.

Clearly illegal content may be reported to the competent authorities.

Your content

The messages, photos and group names you post remain yours. You only grant us a free, non-exclusive permission to store them and display them to the members of your group, to the extent necessary to operate Tribos and for as long as they are kept. We do not use them for other purposes, and in particular not for advertising.

You warrant that you have the right to post what you post, including photos showing other people.

A group’s photo and name are shared elements: they stay attached to the group for as long as it exists, even if you leave it or delete your account.

Health and safety

The programs and sessions offered by Tribos are general training content. They do not constitute medical advice and do not replace that of a health professional.

Adapt your effort to your fitness level, stop in case of pain or discomfort, and ask a doctor for advice if in doubt, especially if you have a health problem.

Deleting your account

You can stop using Tribos at any time and delete your account from the app: Settings › Zone de danger › Supprimer le compte. To open Settings, tap your profile picture on the home screen, then the gear icon.

The details of the data erased are on the delete your account page.

Suspension and exclusion

We may suspend or delete, without notice, the account of a user who breaks these terms, in particular the rules of conduct.
