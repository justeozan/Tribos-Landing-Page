Support

A question about the app, a problem with your account, someone to report? Write to us and we’ll get back to you.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Write to us

One address for everything: evey.run@gmail.com.

  • A question or a problem: we usually reply within two business days.
  • Abusive content or behavior: every report is reviewed within 24 hours.
  • A request about your personal data: we reply within one month at most, as the GDPR requires.

To help us help you faster, include the email address of your Tribos account, your phone model and, if you can, a screenshot of the problem. Never send us your password.

FAQ

How do I join a group with a code?

Ask a member of the group for the invite code. They can find it by tapping the group banner, then “Inviter au groupe”, which copies the code.

If you don’t have a group yet, open the Groupe tab, on the right of the bottom bar, tap “Rejoindre avec un code”, enter the six characters, then tap “Rejoindre”. Uppercase or lowercase, it doesn’t matter. Codes never contain the characters I, L, O, 0 or 1: if you think you see one, it’s a different character.

How do I change program?

Open the Programme tab, in the middle of the bottom bar, scroll all the way down and tap “Changer de programme”. Pick a program from the catalog: it becomes your current program.

Sessions you’ve already completed still count toward your points and toward your group’s weekly goal.

How do I report or block someone?

In the app, you can report a message or a member of your group by choosing a reason: the report is sent to us. You can also block a member: their messages are then hidden for you.

Every report is reviewed within 24 hours. If the content breaks the terms of use, we remove it and exclude its author. You can also write to us directly at evey.run@gmail.com.

How do I delete my account?

On the home screen, tap your profile picture, then the gear icon at the top right to open Paramètres. Scroll to the very bottom, under “Zone de danger”, tap “Supprimer le compte”, type SUPPRIMER to confirm, then tap “Supprimer”.

Deletion is permanent. What is erased, and the little that is kept, is detailed on the Delete your account page.

What happens to my personal data?

We collect the minimum needed to run the app: your account, your username, your sessions and what you share with your group. No ads, no resale, no trackers.

Everything is detailed in the privacy policy, including your rights and how to exercise them.
